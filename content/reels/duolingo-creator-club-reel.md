## 1. Voiceover script (~95 words, about 32–36 s at a relaxed pace)

Read it like you're telling a friend at the kitchen table, not like an announcer. Pauses are marked with `/`.

> **[0:00]** "$1,000 in 28 days?" / *(beat)* That's what stopped my scroll too.
>
> **[0:04]** So I went and looked it up. / This is Duolingo, the language app with the little green owl. / They've got a contract listing called **Duo's Creator Club.**
>
> **[0:12]** Here's what it actually says: / **$1,000 for 28 videos.** / And there may be extra bonuses, depending on how your videos perform.
>
> **[0:20]** Now, to be clear, / I don't work for Duolingo. I'm not recruiting anybody. / I just read the listing, and I think you should too. / Check the requirements. See if it fits you.
>
> **[0:30]** You don't have to call yourself an influencer / to read the fine print. / Link's in the caption.

**Delivery notes**
- Line 1: say it like you're reading it off your phone, a little skeptical. Skepticism builds trust with a 35–55 audience that has seen plenty of "make money online" scams.
- "Here's what it *actually* says": slow down and lean on "actually." That's the turn from rumor to evidence.
- The disclaimer comes at 0:20 on purpose. It's early enough to build trust and late enough not to kill the hook.
- Record in a closet or a car for a dry, close sound. Don't add reverb.

## 2. Visual system

| Element | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Warm newsprint off-white `#F2EEE6` and ink black `#111111` | Reads as editorial or found-document, not as an ad. Highest contrast on any phone. |
| Accent | Highlighter yellow `#F5FF4F` used **only** as a marker swipe behind key numbers | Feels like someone marked up a document. One accent, used sparingly. |
| Secondary accent | Receipt red `#E0352B` for one word only ("ACTUALLY") | Editorial correction-mark energy |
| Headline type | **Anton** or **Archivo Black** (free, Google Fonts), all caps, tight tracking (-1 to -2%) | Condensed and heavy, readable at thumbnail size |
| Body / label type | **IBM Plex Mono** Medium, 38–44 px | Typewriter "case file" feel for labels, quotes and URLs |
| Grain | 3–5% film grain over everything, plus a soft vignette | Takes the digital polish off |
| Brand | Duolingo name and the real listing screenshot only. No floating logos and no owl graphic beyond what's naturally in the screenshot. | You're showing evidence, not co-branding |

**Safe zones (1080 × 1920):** keep all text inside **x 60–900 px, y 250–1450 px**. The bottom ~470 px holds the caption and username, the right ~180 px holds the Like/Share buttons, and the top ~250 px holds Reels UI.

**Real-footage B-roll you shoot yourself (no stock):**
- **A.** Your hand holding your phone, screen showing the listing. Shoot over the shoulder at a kitchen table or on the couch, in window light.
- **B.** Close-up of your thumb scrolling the listing, shot on a second phone or with a screen recording composited in.
- **C.** Optional: you at the table, cropped chest-down (no face needed), coffee mug, notebook with "$1,000 ÷ 28 = $35.71" written by hand.

These read as "someone discovered this." Stock footage can't fake it.

## 4. Screenshot placement and animation (exact specs)

Prep in any editor (CapCut, Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci). Use your **original full-resolution screenshot** and don't recompress it first.

### Shot 1: Listing header "paper card" (0:07–0:12)
1. Crop the screenshot to the **job title and company block** only.
2. Scale it so the card is **900 px wide** (centered, x = 90). The height follows the crop.
3. Card treatment: 0 px corner radius (it's a document, not an app card), **drop shadow** black at 35% opacity, 0 px X / 18 px Y offset, 40 px blur. Rotate the card **-2°**.
4. Position the card's center at **y = 820**.
5. **Animation:**
   - Frames 0–8: drops in from y = 760 to 820 and rotation -6° → -2°, ease-out. Fade in 0→100% over 4 frames.
   - Frames 8–150: continuous drift, scale 100 → 104%, rotation -2° → -1°, linear.
   - At ~0:10.5: a black **underline** (8 px thick) draws left→right under "Creator Club" over 8 frames.
6. Background behind the card: the newsprint off-white with grain.

### Shot 2: Pay line "evidence crop" (0:14–0:20)
1. Duplicate the screenshot. Crop tight to the **sentence with the $1,000 / 28 videos** and the **bonus sentence** underneath it.
2. Place it **full-bleed width (1080 px)**, scaled so the pay text is at least **64 px tall on screen**. Upscale if needed and add a light sharpen (amount 20–30).
3. Position the pay line at **y ≈ 760**.
4. **Animation:**
   - Frame 0: **punch-in** cut from 115% to 100% over 6 frames (ease-out). It feels like the camera "finds" the line.
   - Frames 6–16: **highlighter swipe**, a yellow `#F5FF4F` rectangle at 85% opacity in **Multiply** blend mode, tall enough to cover the text line plus 10 px. Mask it so it reveals left→right over 10 frames. Give the leading edge a 6 px feather and skew the rectangle by -3° so it looks hand-drawn.
   - 0:15: big overlay **$1,000 → 28 VIDEOS** (Anton 140 px, black on a white band) slides up from y = 1350 to 1250 above the safe-zone floor.
   - 0:18: **pan the screenshot up** so the bonus line moves to y ≈ 760 (12 frames, ease-in-out). A second highlighter swipe follows on the bonus line.
5. Dim everything outside the highlighted lines to 55% brightness, or add a 40% black overlay with a cutout. That directs the eye.

### Shot 3: Requirements scroll (0:25–0:30)
1. Use your **screen recording** of the official listing. Trim it to the requirements or eligibility section.
2. Place it inside a **phone-shaped mask**: 820 × 1640 px, 60 px corner radius, centered at y = 900, with a 6 px black border. Or show it full-screen with a 6% vignette if the recording is already clean.
3. Speed: **1.0–1.25×.** Don't speed-ramp. A natural scroll reads as authentic.
4. At the most important eligibility line (location, age, platform, or follower minimum), **freeze-frame for 1 s** and draw a hand-drawn red circle (`#E0352B`, 8 px stroke, slightly imperfect path). Animate the stroke on over 10 frames.
5. Before posting, **blur any personal info** in the recording (your email, notifications, other tabs).

### General rules for all screenshots
- Never fake or retype listing text. Show the real pixels. The screenshot is your credibility.
- Keep the listing URL visible at least once (Shot 1 or B-roll A).
- Don't add a Duolingo logo on top. If the owl appears in the screenshot itself, that's enough.

## 5. Closing screen (0:30–0:35)

**Background:** ink black `#111111` with grain. **Layout** (all inside the safe zone):

```
y=380   READ IT                  ← Anton 170 px, off-white
y=560   YOURSELF.                ← Anton 170 px, off-white
y=760   ───────────────          ← 4 px yellow rule, 300 px wide
y=840   $1,000 · 28 VIDEOS       ← Plex Mono 54 px, yellow #F5FF4F
y=920   + possible bonuses       ← Plex Mono 40 px, 70% white
y=1100  LINK IN CAPTION ↓        ← Plex Mono 46 px, off-white, slow 2-cycle pulse (100→60% opacity)
y=1300  Not affiliated with Duolingo. Just sharing what I found.
                                 ← Plex Mono 30 px, 60% white
```

**Build:** "READ IT" hard-cuts on with the VO line, "YOURSELF." follows 6 frames later, and the yellow rule draws on over 8 frames. The rest fades in together over 6 frames. Hold for at least 2 seconds with nothing moving except the "LINK" pulse.

**Why this closer:** "Read it yourself" puts the decision in the viewer's hands. That's the opposite of a hard sell, and it's the line people will remember and repeat in comments.
